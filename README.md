## **About**  
**Entropica** is a **magic and technology mod** that blends mystical energies with industrial advancement to create a unique gameplay experience in Minecraft.  

Featuring a planned **spellcrafting system**, players can explore the synergy between **life essence** and **technological innovation** to fuel their progress.  

---

## **Features**  
- **Multiblock Structures**: Build advanced systems like the **Vis Fume Pressure Vessel** to store and manage mystical fumes.  
- **Balanced Fusion of Magic and Technology**: Harness life essence to power intricate machines and spell systems.  
- **Planned Spellcrafting**: A robust and highly customizable spellcrafting mechanic is in development.  
- **Diagnostics**: Interactive tools deliver real-time feedback for players about their multiblock systems and stored resources.  
- **Seamless Networking**: Multiplayer support to transport and manage resources efficiently.  

---

## **Installation**  
Follow these steps to install **Entropica**:  
1. Download the latest version of the **`entropica-x.x.x.jar`** file from the [Releases](#) section of this repository.  
2. Ensure you have Minecraft with the appropriate mod loader (**Forge**) installed.  
3. Place the `.jar` file in the `mods` folder of your Minecraft directory.  
4. Launch the game, and enjoy exploring **Entropica's** world!
