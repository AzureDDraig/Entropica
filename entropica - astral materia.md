### 1.1 The 3D Volumetric Stellar Tears
In Entropica’s cosmology, the universe is enclosed within a crystalline dimensional firmament that separates physical reality from the primordial plane—an infinite ocean of pure, undifferentiated Materia.

Distant stars are not burning balls of plasma, nor are they flat 2D projections. They are **colossal three-dimensional volumetric tears in the cosmic firmament**. 
- Each star acts as a **positive-pressure cosmic fountain**, continuously pumping primal Materia directly into the vacuum of space.
- As this energy radiates across deep space, it travels in coherent, geometrically structured harmonic currents known as **Stellar Weaves**.
- **No Frequency Tiering**: The energy pouring from these tears is pure, fundamental **Materia**—the exact same essence utilized in terrestrial Entropica alchemy, pneumatic machines, and Orbis Cells.
- **Altitude Independence**: The physical height ($Y$-level) of an observer, collector, or altar does not affect the density or gathering speed of stellar Materia. Starlight falls uniformly upon the earth.

---

### 1.2 The Day Star (Sun), Solar Glare & Focus Persistence
The **Sun (Day Star)** is metaphysically identical to the distant night stars: it is simply the closest and most massive 3D tear in the firmament.

```
Night Sky (Vacuum Clarity)    ──► Low Ambient Noise ──► Coherent Constellation Weaves Focused
Day Sky (Solar Radiance Glare)──► Chaotic Ambient Wash──► Blinds Instruments to New Constellations
                                                       └── Maintains Existing Locked Foci!
```

- **Daytime Atmospheric Glare**: During the day, the overwhelming proximity of the Sun floods the sky with intense ambient heat, light, and chaotic Materia scattering. This solar glare blinds optical instruments, preventing astronomers from locating or acquiring *new* constellation foci during daylight hours.
- **Focus Persistence Mechanics**: When an Astral Collector, Observatory, or Refractive Lens establishes an optical lock on a constellation during the night, **that locked focus remains active throughout the daytime**. The established harmonic link channels stellar Materia uninterrupted through the day.
- **Unlinking Penalty**: If a player moves, re-aims, or breaks the focus of an instrument during the day, that constellation focus is immediately severed. Because the solar glare obscures the heavens, the constellation cannot be re-acquired or re-focused until the night sky returns, even if the player has the exact celestial coordinates recorded!

### 1.3 The Cosmic Void & Universal Entropy Equilibrium
Cosmology in Entropica is built upon a fundamental energetic dualism:

| Cosmic Element | Metaphysical Nature | Pressure Dynamic | Role in the Universe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3D Stellar Tears (Stars)** | Primordial Fountains | **Positive-Pressure Injection** | Pumps pure Materia into physical reality; generates structure, heat, and life. |
| **The Cosmic Void ($y < -64$)** | Primordial Abyss | **Negative-Pressure Sink** | Pulls Materia out of reality back into the abyss; maintains universal equilibrium (Entropy). |

The Cosmic Void below bedrock ($y < -64$) and in the deep inter-dimensional gulfs acts as a massive negative-pressure sink. Without the continuous influx of pure Materia pouring from the 3D Stellar Tears, physical reality would dissolve into entropic stillness. Conversely, without the Cosmic Void drawing excess essence out of the universe, reality would suffer catastrophic energetic overpressure.

*(Note: In-game localized Void Rifts generated by the Void Tuning Fork are logistical teleportation features and are distinct from the true cosmological Void sink).*

### 1.4 Dimensional Sky Visibility: Overworld, The End, and The Nether

```
[ Overworld ] ──► Standard Day/Night Cycle + 8 Moon Phases (Dictates Tier Visibility)
[ The End ]   ──► Pure Vacuum Firmament (All 24 Constellations Visible Permanently)
[ The Nether ]──► Solid Bedrock Ceiling (0% Starlight; Requires Optic Fibers to Channel Down)
```

1. **The Overworld**:
   - Features a dynamic day/night cycle and an 8-phase lunar cycle ($0 \dots 7$).
   - The lunar phase strictly dictates which tiers and categories of constellations are visible and focusable in the night sky.
2. **The End**:
   - Exists in a deep vacuum void devoid of atmosphere and sun.
   - **All 24 Constellations are permanently visible simultaneously** in The End at all times, making it the ultimate sovereign realm for celestial harvesting and cosmic rituals.
3. **The Nether**:
   - Enclosed beneath an unbroken ceiling of ancient bedrock.
   - The bedrock roof completely blocks all celestial starlight; no constellations can be viewed and no natural starlight falls within the Nether.
   - Starlight can only operate in the Nether if players construct surface collectors in the Overworld and channel starlight down through **Pure Optic Fibers** across dimensions.

### 1.5 Atmospheric & Weather Interference Dynamics
Weather conditions in the Overworld create temporary optical attenuation:
- **Clear Night Sky**: 100% optical transmission; all eligible constellations are visible and can be charted or focused at maximum flux.
- **Rain, Snow & Thunderstorms**: Dense cloud cover obscures un-focused constellations from being charted or locked onto with telescopes.
- **Persistent Focus During Storms**: Any Astral Collector or Lens that had already established a locked focus on a constellation prior to the storm **continues to harvest stellar Materia**, operating with a minor **25% atmospheric attenuation penalty** until the storm clears.

### 2.1 The 4-Tier Hierarchical Classification
1. **Tier 1: Fundamental Constellations (8)**:
   - Simple geometric patterns composed of **3 to 5 stars**.
   - Visible during **Crescent & Quarter Moons** (Phases 2, 3, 5, 6).
   - Discoverable using the Tier-1 **Handheld Looking Glass**.
   - Grants core survivability, basic elemental masteries, and patron soul attunement.
2. **Tier 2: Advanced Constellations (8)**:
   - Moderately complex geometric patterns of **5 to 8 stars**.
   - Visible during **Gibbous & Full Moons** (Phases 0, 1, 7).
   - Discoverable using the Tier-2 **Astrolabe & Drafting Compass** (requires at least 1 Tier-1 attunement).
   - Grants industrial machine speedups, resource multipliers, and advanced combat traits.
3. **Tier 3: Master Constellations (4)**:
   - Complex geometric star networks of **8 to 11 stars** with shared-node junctions.
   - Visible exclusively during **Peak Full Moon Zenith** (Phase 0).
   - Discoverable using the Tier-3 **Stationary Brass Telescope** (requires at least 1 Tier-2 unlock).
   - Grants high-tier multiblock ritual drivers, gravitational fields, and duplication chances.
4. **Tier 4: Mythic Cosmic Entities (4)**:
   - Expansive celestial geometries of **11 to 15 stars** with heavy shared-node overlap.
   - Visible exclusively during **New Moon (Phase 4)** in the Overworld and permanently in **The End**.
   - Discoverable using the Tier-4 **7x7x6 Grand Circular Astral Observatory** (requires at least 1 Tier-3 unlock).
   - Grants reality-warping masteries: true creative flight, death immunity, and celestial portals.
