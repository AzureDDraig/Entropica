# Vis Logistics

Moving magical energy from where it is generated to where it is needed is a core challenge in Entropica. Energy exists in two primary states for transport: **Vis Vitae** (Liquid) and **Vis Fume** (Gas).

---

## **Vis Vitae (Liquids)** 💧
Vis Vitae is the liquid form of magical energy. It is relatively stable but heavy to transport over long distances without pumps.

### **Components**
- **Vis Pipe**: The standard transport medium for liquids.
- **Vis Vitae Anchor**: Secures pipes and provides connection points for machines.
- **Vitae Barrel**: A large storage container for liquid essence.
- **Vis Ichor Valve**: Controls the flow rate of liquids.

---

## **Vis Fume (Gases)** 💨
Vis Fume is the gaseous, highly energetic state of essence. It is fast and efficient but requires careful pressure management.

### **The Pressure System**
Fume networks operate on a pressure gradient. High pressure at the source (like a **Pressure Vessel**) drives the gas through the pipes to your machines.
- **Overpressure**: Be careful! Exceeding the pressure limit of your pipes can cause them to burst, leaking toxic fumes into the environment.

### **Components**
- **Vis Fume Pipe**: Reinforced pipes designed for high-pressure gas.
- **Vis Fume Valve**: Precise control for gas flow.
- **Vis Fume One-Way Valve**: Prevents backflow in complex networks.
- **Vis Fume Diverter**: Splts or merges gas streams.
- **Vis Fume Condenser**: Converts high-pressure Vis Fume back into stable liquid Vis Vitae.

---

## **Safety & Diagnostics**
- **Vis Exhaust**: A vital safety component. Install these to vent excess pressure automatically.
- **Vis Value Detector**: Use this on pipes or machines to check current pressure and energy levels.
- **Vis Toxicity**: Being exposed to leaked Vis Fumes can cause the **Vis Toxicity** status effect, which is dangerous if not treated.

---

## **Network Management**
For large-scale setups, use the **Aetheric Terminal** to monitor your entire network from a single interface.
