# Getting Started with Entropica

Your journey into Entropica begins with understanding the basic interaction between magic and the physical world.

---

## **1. The First Step: Diluted Essence**
The most basic form of magical energy you'll encounter is **Diluted Essence**. It is created by transforming common water into a magically reactive fluid.

### **Creation**
To create a pool of Diluted Essence:
1.  Find or create a pool of **Water**.
2.  Toss **Raw Essence** items (harvested from mobs) directly into the water.
3.  The water will transform into a glowing, cyan **Diluted Essence Fluid**.

### **Transformation**
Once you have a pool of Diluted Essence, you can toss specific items into it to trigger transformations. This is your primary way of obtaining early-game magical materials.

---

## **2. Harvesting Life Essence**
To progress, you need to collect the essence of living things.

### **The Essence Harvesting Blade**
Crafting an **Essence Harvesting Blade** is essential. This tool allows you to reap essence more effectively from defeated foes.
- **Essence Reap Enchantment**: This enchantment can be applied to weapons to increase the amount of essence dropped by mobs.

### **Storing Essence**
Collected essence comes in three main strengths:
- **Weak Essence** (Early game)
- **Average Essence** (Mid game)
- **Strong Essence** (Late game)

These can be stored in **Ampoules** (Small, Medium, Large) for later use in machines.

---

## **3. Your First Tools**
- **Arcanum Focus**: A basic tool used to interact with various magical blocks and systems.
- **Vis Value Detector**: Use this to see how much magical energy (Vis) is contained within items and blocks.

---

## **4. What's Next?**
Once you have a steady supply of essence and have begun transforming materials, you should look into building your first **Mana Furnace** or **Aetheric Synthesizer** to begin more advanced crafting.
