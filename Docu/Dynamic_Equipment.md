# Dynamic Equipment

Dynamic Equipment represents the pinnacle of Entropica's craftsmanship. Instead of static, unchangeable tools, you create modular items that can be customized with cores, shapes, and magical modifiers.

---

## **The Forging Process**

All dynamic equipment is forged at the **Eidolic Lathe**. The process requires several key components to be placed in the lathe and its surrounding pedestals.

### **1. Central Components**
You must place these in the center of the Eidolic Lathe:
- **Shape Concept**: Defines the form of the item (e.g., *Gladius*, *Pickaxe*, *Longbow*).
- **Core**: The heart of the item. Cores come in Arcanite, Viscanite, Resonite, and Eidolite varieties, each providing different base stats.
- **Orbis Acceptor**: Acts as the intake valve for the magical energy required to power the item.
- **Base Material**: 4 Ingots of your chosen metal (Arcanite, Viscanite, etc.) to form the physical body.

### **2. Modifiers (Attunement Pedestals)**
You can surround the lathe with up to **16 Attunement Pedestals**. Items placed here will modify the final product:
- **Spell Gems**: Imbue the item with active or passive magical abilities.
- **Quartz**: Increases sharpness or efficiency.
- **Feathers**: Reduces weight and increases speed.
- **Glowstone**: Adds innate luminescence or radiant damage.

### **3. Powering the Forge**
Forging a dynamic item requires **Vis**. You must pipe between **500 and 100,000 Vis** into the Lathe to begin the process. The more complex the modifiers, the more Vis is required.

## **Modular Cores**
The core you choose determines the item's fundamental behavior:
- **Arcanite Core**: High magical affinity, great for spell-focused weapons.
- **Viscanite Core**: High durability and physical damage.
- **Resonite Core**: High speed and kinetic force.
- **Eidolite Core**: Grants "spectral intelligence," allowing for autonomous behaviors or homing.

---

## **Customization & Upgrading**
Dynamic items can often be returned to the lathe or an **Arcane Anvil** to have their modifiers swapped or their cores upgraded (e.g., from a standard core to a **Charged** or **Ancient** core).
