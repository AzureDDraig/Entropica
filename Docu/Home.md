# Entropica Wiki

![Entropica Logo](https://github.com/AzureDDraig/Entropica/blob/master/image_2026-02-28_155722900.png?raw=true)

Welcome to the official wiki for **Entropica**, a Minecraft mod that merges the mystical arts with industrial engineering. In Entropica, life essence isn't just a byproduct of existence—it's the fuel for your next great invention.

---

## **Introduction**
**Entropica** focuses on the synergy between **Life Essence** (Vis) and **Technological Progress**. Whether you're forging powerful dynamic weapons or managing complex gas-pressure networks, Entropica offers a deep, multi-layered experience.

### **Core Pillars**
- **Essence Manipulation**: Harvest, refine, and store the fundamental energies of the world.
- **Advanced Logistics**: Move energy through liquid (Vis Vitae) and gas (Vis Fume) states using a variety of pipes, valves, and condensers.
- **Dynamic Equipment**: Forge tools and weapons that are truly yours, using a modular system of cores, shapes, and spell gems.
- **Industrial Multiblocks**: Build massive structures to process high-pressure mystical fumes.
