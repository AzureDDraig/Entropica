# Materials & Components

Entropica features four primary magical alloys and metals, each with its own unique properties and uses in the mod's industrial and magical systems.

---

## **The Four Pillars**

### **1. Arcanite** 💎
*The definitive magical conductor.*
- **Appearance**: Cyan-tinted metal.
- **Role**: Arcanite is the backbone of most magical circuitry and logic systems. It has the highest conductivity for raw magical energy.
- **Key Components**: Arcanite Wire, Arcanite Filament, Arcanite Frame.

### **2. Viscanite** 🔩
*A dense, weapon-grade industrial alloy.*
- **Appearance**: Dark gunmetal.
- **Role**: Built for durability and force, Viscanite is used in heavy-duty machinery and high-impact weaponry. It excels at handling kinetic and explosive magical pressures.
- **Key Components**: Viscanite Piston, Viscanite Stator, Viscanite Gearset.

### **3. Resonite** 🔔
*An acoustic alloy tuned to invisible frequencies.*
- **Appearance**: Polished gold and brass.
- **Role**: Resonite is used for precision engineering and frequency manipulation. It is essential for machines that rely on vibration and acoustic pressure waves.
- **Key Components**: Tuning Fork, Resonite Stator, Resonite Plate.

### **4. Eidolite** 👻
*A pale, ghostly silver metal retaining spectral intelligence.*
- **Appearance**: Pale, glowing silver.
- **Role**: Eidolite is a semi-autonomous metal used for advanced, "smart" machinery and spectral equipment. It often moves or reacts on its own.
- **Key Components**: Eidolite Core, Eidolite Ichor Valve, Eidolite Piston.

---

## **Processing States**
Materials often need to be processed into different forms for use in recipes:

| Form | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ingot** | The standard solid form of the metal. |
| **Nugget** | Smaller pieces used for delicate work like wires. |
| **Plate** | Flattened sheets used for armor and machine casings. |
| **Wire / Filament** | Conductive strands used in logic gates and spools. |
| **Gear / Gearset** | Used for mechanical torque and rotational power. |

---

## **Charging Materials**
Many materials can be **Charged** to enhance their properties. This usually involves processing them in an **Aetheric Synthesizer** with specific **Vis Fumes**.

- **Charged Arcanite**: Overclocked for higher energy throughput.
- **Charged Viscanite**: Imbued with massive kinetic potential.
- **Charged Resonite**: Humming with hyper-dense vibrations.
- **Charged Eidolite**: Heightened phantom activity and intelligence.
