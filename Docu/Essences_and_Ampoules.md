# Essences & Ampoules

Life essence is the fundamental fuel of Entropica. It exists in many flavors, each associated with a different aspect of the world.

---

## **Types of Essence**
Essence is categorized by its elemental or conceptual affinity. Different affinities are required for different recipes and machine processes.

| Affinity | Sources (Examples) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Undead** | Zombies, Skeletons |
| **Water** | Fish, Guardians, Squids |
| **Nether** | Blazes, Ghasts, Piglins |
| **Chimera** | Rare, multi-aspected creatures |
| **Arid** | Husks, Desert life |
| **Frozen** | Strays, Tundra life |
| **Void** | Endermen, Shulkers |
| **Air** | Phantoms, Flying creatures |
| **Earth** | Golems, Underground life |
| **Nature** | Animals, Plants |
| **Lightning** | Creepers (Charged), Stormy events |
| **Radiant** | Light-bearing creatures |
| **Umbral** | Shadow-dwelling creatures |

---

## **Essence Strengths**
As you progress, you will find or refine essence into higher tiers of potency:
1.  **Weak Essence**: The most common form, dropped by basic mobs.
2.  **Average Essence**: More potent, obtained from tougher foes or through refining.
3.  **Strong Essence**: Highly concentrated energy, used in endgame machinery.

---

## **Ampoules**
To transport and use essence (especially in its gaseous **Vis Fume** state), you must use **Ampoules**.

- **Small Ampoule**: Holds a small amount of essence/fume.
- **Medium Ampoule**: Increased capacity.
- **Large Ampoule**: The maximum storage capacity for a single handheld container.

### **Using Ampoules**
Ampoules are often placed into **Receptacles** in machines (like the **Mana Furnace**) or used as inputs in the **Aetheric Synthesizer**.

---

## **Vis Fumes**
When essence is processed, it often transitions into a gaseous state known as **Vis Fume**. Fumes are highly volatile and must be managed using pressurized networks.
