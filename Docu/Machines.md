# Machines & Automation

Entropica provides a suite of advanced machinery to process materials, generate power, and automate complex tasks.

---

## **Core Machinery**

### **1. Mana Furnace** 🔥
The starting point for energy generation. The Mana Furnace consumes **Essence** (in ampoules or raw form) to generate **Mana** or heat for other processes.
- **Components**: Essence Receptacle, Catalyst Receptacle, Furnace Hatch.

### **2. Aetheric Synthesizer** 🧪
A complex machine used for high-tier crafting. It requires specific **Vis Fumes** and items to synthesize advanced materials like **Charged Arcanite**.
- **Interface**: Uses the **Synthesizer User Interface** block to manage recipes and inputs.

### **3. Eidolic Lathe** ⚔️
The primary workstation for creating **Dynamic Equipment**. It uses a central Shape Concept, a Core, and an Orbis Acceptor to forge weapons and tools.
- **Power**: Requires between 500 and 100,000 Vis to operate.

### **4. Morphic Loom** 🧶
Used for weaving spectral materials and perhaps preparing fabrics or strings imbued with magical properties.

### **5. Aetheric Terminal** 🖥️
The central hub for managing your magical networks. It provides an overview of your stored Vis and active systems.

---

## **Industrial Tools**

- **Entropic Auto-Smelter**: A high-efficiency furnace that utilizes entropic energy to process ores rapidly.
- **Vis Motor**: Converts magical energy into mechanical torque for driving pistons and gears.
- **Vis Extraction Apparatus**: Extracts raw Vis from the environment or specific nodes.
- **Enrichment Table**: Enhances the properties of certain items by bathing them in concentrated essence.
- **Arcane Anvil**: A specialized anvil for working with magical alloys without losing their spectral integrity.

---

## **Network Components**

- **Vis Readout / Essence Readout**: Interactive displays that show current storage levels.
- **Vis Exhaust**: Safely vents excess magical pressure to prevent system failure.
- **Aetheric Automator**: Can be configured to automate repetitive tasks within your magical workshop.

---

## **Machine Tiers**
Many machines come in different tiers or can be upgraded:
- **Simple Machine Block**: Used for basic devices.
- **Complex Machine Block**: Required for advanced, multi-stage machinery.
