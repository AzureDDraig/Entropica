# Runes

Runes are ancient symbols imbued with conceptual power. In Entropica, they serve as the bridge between raw magical energy and specific, directed outcomes.

---

## **Using Runes**
Runestones are often used as:
- **Crafting Ingredients**: For high-tier components like the **Spectral Glyph-Plate**.
- **Modifiers**: In the **Eidolic Lathe** to add specific conceptual properties to Dynamic Equipment.
- **Spellcrafting**: (Planned) Runes will form the alphabet of the spellcasting system.

---

## **The Void Rift**
The **Void Rift** is a powerful, dangerous phenomenon associated with high-level runic activity. It can be stabilized or utilized by those with enough knowledge of the darker runes.
