# Multiblock Structures

For high-end processing and large-scale storage, single blocks aren't enough. Entropica utilizes **Multiblock Structures**—large, custom-built machines made of multiple different blocks.

---

## **1. Vis Fume Pressure Vessel** 🛢️
The Pressure Vessel is a massive storage tank for **Vis Fumes**. Its capacity and durability depend on the materials used in its construction.

### **Construction**
- **Controller**: The **Vis Fume Pressure Vessel Controller** block.
- **Walls**: Built using **Arcane Bricks** or **Arcanite Plating**.
- **Windows**: Use specialized glass like **Essence Enriched Glass** or **Vis Fume Strengthened Glass** to see the fumes inside.
- **Ports**: Use **Vis Fume Pressure Vessel Ports** to connect pipes.

### **Glass Tiers**
The type of glass you use affects the vessel's capacity:
- **Essence Enriched Glass**: Standard capacity.
- **Strengthened Glass**: Higher pressure resistance.
- **Ichor Enriched Glass**: High capacity.
- **Fragment Lattice Glass**: Maximum capacity and stability.

---

## **2. Vis Fume Pressure Chamber** 🔬
A high-pressure environment used for specialized crafting and material transformation.

### **Usage**
- Items are placed inside the chamber via an input port.
- The chamber is filled with high-pressure Vis Fumes.
- After a certain amount of time (ticks), the items are transformed into their new state and ejected via an output port.

---

## **Maintenance & Safety**
Multiblocks are powerful but can be volatile.
- **Diagnostics**: Always use a **Vis Value Detector** to ensure your multiblock is formed correctly.
- **Integrity**: Damaging even a single block of a multiblock structure will cause it to "de-form," potentially leaking all stored energy or fumes.
